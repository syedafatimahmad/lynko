Privacy Policy

How we handle, protect, and respect your data when you use the Lynko application.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Lynko mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "the App" or "Service"), developed and operated by Lynko, Inc. ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy outlines our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

While using our Application, we may collect certain personally identifiable information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: When you register or authenticate through Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and unique user identifier (UID).
  • Location Data (Approximate & Precise): With your permission, we access your device's location to allow you to pin, tag, and verify project site locations and environmental sampling addresses on the interactive map.
  • Camera & Photo Library: With your permission, we access your camera and photo library to allow you to take and attach site inspection photos, sample documentation, and Chain of Custody visual records.
  • Field Inspection & Chain of Custody Records: Data entered during project workflows, including project numbers, sample IDs, sample types, environmental descriptions, signatures, and timestamps.

Notice: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or inspection data to third parties or advertising networks.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for specific, legitimate business purposes:

  • To create and maintain your user account.
  • To generate and export Chain of Custody PDF reports and inspection documentation.
  • To enable GPS-assisted site tagging and map-based sample location verification.
  • To synchronize your projects and inspection drafts securely across your authorized devices via cloud storage.
  • To communicate essential service updates, account verification links, and customer support responses.

3. Device Permissions Requested

To deliver core field inspection functionality, Lynko requests the following Android permissions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely when you take photos of site locations or sample containers to attach to Chain of Custody records.
  • Storage / Media: Used solely to select existing inspection photos from your gallery or save generated PDF reports to your device.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used solely to identify your current position on the map when recording sample collection coordinates. You can choose not to grant location permissions and manually enter addresses.

4. Third-Party Services & Processors

We work with trusted third-party infrastructure providers who process data strictly under our instructions and in compliance with this Privacy Policy:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: Provides secure authentication (Firebase Auth), real-time encrypted cloud database (Cloud Firestore), and media storage (Firebase Storage). Review Google's Privacy Policy.
  • LocationIQ & Google Maps: Used for map display and address geocoding searches.

5. Data Security & Storage

The security of your data is paramount to us:

  • All communications between the App and our backend servers are strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • User data and documents stored in Firebase are protected by granular Firestore and Storage security rules, ensuring only authenticated and authorized users can access project records.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

We retain your personal and project information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or as required by environmental compliance records.

How to request account and data deletion:

You have the full right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. To request deletion, simply send an email to:

Email your request to: thelynkoapp@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request - [Your Registered Email]

Upon receipt, your user profile and associated personal records will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is designed strictly for professionals and inspectors and is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of the policy.
