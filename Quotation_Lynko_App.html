2. Granular Feature & Native Integration Specification
Module 1: Authentication & Account Management
  • Native Google Play Services Integration: 1-tap sign-in powered by official Google Play Services SDK (@react-native-google-signin/google-signin) with zero browser redirect latency.
  • Native Account Chooser Modal: Automatic session clearance to present the full Google Account Picker bottom-sheet, allowing inspectors to select any device account or add new credentials.
  • Email / Password Authentication: Direct Firebase Auth engine with automatic account creation, password reset workflows, and secure session management.
  • Automated Email Verification: Dedicated verification dispatch and delivery tracking via custom SMTP integration (thelynkoapp@gmail.com).
  • Persistent Local Auth Session: Encrypted token storage with AsyncStorage for instant app cold-starts without re-login requirements.
Module 2: Projects & Inspection Lifecycle Dashboard
  • Unified 3-Tab Architecture: Clean segregation between All, Drafts (in-progress field jobs), and Submitted (finalized & sent to lab) inspection records.
  • Instant Search Engine: Real-time filtering across PO numbers, project titles, physical site addresses, descriptions, and sampling dates.
  • Status Visual Badging: High-visibility badges clearly distinguishing active field drafts from finalized lab submissions.
  • One-Tap PDF Viewer: Direct access to view and print the official signed Chain of Custody PDF directly from any project card.
  • Safe Record Management: Touch-optimized deletion with safety confirmation dialogs and automatic Firestore synchronization.
Module 3: EPA Digital Chain of Custody & Sample Matrix
  • 9 Specialized Media Presets: Pre-configured tracking for Asbestos PCM Cassettes, Asbestos TEM, Bulk Samples (Drywall, Tile, Mastic), Endotoxin, Polycarbonate Air Filters, PTFE Membrane Filters, Spore Trap Cassettes (Air-O-Cell), Surface Tape Slides, and Viable Via-Cell Cassettes.
  • Batch Stepper Counter: Interactive SampleTypesScreen stepper allowing inspectors to tap +/- on physical cassettes in their field kit to auto-generate sample rows.
  • Dual-Tier Analysis & Turnaround Management: Global analysis configuration (e.g. Asbestos PLM, TEM Analysis) and rush turnaround selection (e.g. Next-day rush, 2-Hour Emergency).
  • Granular Sample Fields: Sample ID, Room / Location Description, Material Property, Flow Volume Measurement & Units (L, pc, in²), and individual Analysis 1 & 2 toggles.
  • Batch Auto-Fill Productivity Suite: 1-tap sequential re-numbering (1, 2, 3...), mass area description population, and measurement auto-fill.
Module 4: Hardware Camera & High-Resolution Photo Engine
  • Native Camera Capture: Direct hardware integration via expo-camera to document building conditions and sampling locations on-site.
  • Photo Library Integration: Multi-photo selector via expo-image-picker for attaching existing building inspection photos.
  • Photo Grid Management: Visual thumbnail gallery with numbered badges (#1, #2...) and large touch-target removal controls.
  • Full-Resolution Lab Attachment: Automatically bundles all site photos alongside the official PDF document during email lab submission.
Module 5: Digital Signature Capture & EPA PDF Engine
  • Vector Signature Canvas: In-app touch/stylus signature modal (react-native-signature-canvas) with instant clearing and base64 export.
  • Dual Legal Signatures: Dedicated capture and binding for both Relinquished By (Field Sampler) and Received By (Laboratory Courier).
  • Multi-Page Vector PDF Compilation: Client-side PDF engine (expo-print) rendering strict EPA/NVLAP laboratory grid tables.
  • Automatic Pagination: Automatically paginates 15 samples per page with synchronized headers, company info, and total sample counters.
  • Native Device Preview & Sharing: Opens PDF directly in Android's native print/PDF viewer for immediate verification.
Module 6: Cloud Synchronization & Automated Lab Email Dispatch
  • Google Firebase Firestore Backend: Encrypted cloud database synchronization under isolated user paths with zero security vulnerability.
  • Offline-First Resilience: Full local persistence (AsyncStorage & Zustand) allowing field inspectors to log samples in zero-reception basements with automatic sync upon re-connection.
  • Automated MailComposer Dispatch: 1-tap email generation pre-populating recipient laboratory, PO subject line, sample counts, PDF attachment, and photo files.
  • Recipient History Memory: Automatically records and suggests frequently dispatched testing lab email addresses (e.g. EMSL, Eurofins, SGS).
